How it works

  1. Upload PDF or DOCX resumes (or paste text).
  2. Choose a role or paste a job description, then confirm the requirements.
  3. Review score breakdown, evidence, gaps, and suggested rewrites.

A skill marked “not found” means the resume doesn’t show it, not that you lack it. Scores come from fixed, visible rules, so you can check them by hand.
